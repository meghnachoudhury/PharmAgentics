# Project national TRx by territory, but never name a driver from a projected number

The release has territory-level counts and a weekly panel coverage per territory, not a pharmacy panel. We report a projected national (observed TRx ÷ panel coverage per territory, summed; a territory below 0.5 coverage is estimated from its own recent share of national) so the brief states a national level every week, as a syndicated weekly report would. Growth stays like-for-like, and a story never names a driver for a projected or estimated cut, because projection corrects a level, not a cause.

## Consequences

Golden cases P1-Q2 and P3-Q6 (the incomplete New England week of 2026-05-04) change: the territory now has a projected level, and its story is still withheld.
